Send this at T-14d, once seats are assigned and repository access is confirmed. Chase any
unconfirmed participant individually at T-7d; a chase to the whole group is ignored by the
whole group.

## Message

> **Subject:** Your Copilot hackathon on {{dates}} — two things to do before we start
>
> You are booked onto the GitHub Copilot Enablement Hackathon on **{{dates}}** at
> **{{location}}**. We will be working in **{{repository}}**, on items from your own backlog,
> so what you build during the three days is yours to keep and to merge.
>
> **Please do these two things before {{date minus 3 days}}:**
>
> 1. **Sign in and check your access.** Open {{repository}}, confirm Copilot is active in your
>    editor, and confirm you can push a branch. If any part of that fails, reply to this mail —
>    it is much cheaper to fix now than on the morning.
> 2. **Bring two problems.** Two things in {{repository}} that annoy you and that you could
>    finish in a day. They do not need to be impressive. Real and small beats interesting.
>
> **Pre-work (about 30 minutes):** skim
> [What is GitHub Copilot](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/what-is-github-copilot).
> You do not need to memorise anything; we start hands-on.
>
> **What to bring:** your normal development machine, set up the way you normally work. Please
> do not bring a clean machine specially — the point is to work in your real environment.

## Prerequisites checklist

| Item | Owner | Verified by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copilot seat assigned and signed in at least once | Participant | T-7d |
| Push access to the working repository | Platform owner | T-7d |
| Able to open a pull request | Participant | T-7d |
| Editor and extension installed | Participant | T-7d |
| Access works from the delivery location's network | Delivery lead | T-7d |

## Notes

- Verify each seat resolves to a person who is actually attending. Seats assigned to a team
  alias are the most common reason a participant cannot sign in on day one.
- Check network access from the delivery location itself, not from the office of whoever is
  organising it.
